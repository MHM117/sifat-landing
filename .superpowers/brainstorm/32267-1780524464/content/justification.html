Our Approach
Fair Pricing, No Tricks
We believe spiritual knowledge shouldn't come with a recurring price tag.
📖
Better Value Than Books
Physical flashcards and books on the Names can cost £30+, yet can't guarantee you'll memorise them. Sifat uses spaced repetition to make sure you do — for a fraction of the price.
✅
A Goal You Can Complete
Unlike a streaming service, Sifat has a finish line. Once you've memorised the Names, you're done. Come back anytime for a refresher — like before Ramadan — but you'll never be locked into paying.
🔒
No Subscriptions. Ever.
We chose one-time pricing because it's the right thing to do. You shouldn't have to worry about cancelling or being charged month after month for knowledge that benefits your akhirah.
🌱
More to Come
We have plans for additional decks — like Quran Surah Names — so your one-time purchase keeps growing in value over time.
