# PRODUCT.md

## Product Purpose

Sifat is an iOS app that helps Muslims memorise and deeply understand the 99 Names of Allah through spaced-repetition flashcards, quizzes, and reflective study. This landing page is the marketing site for the app, live at sifat.app.

## Users

- **Primary**: Muslim adults (20s-40s) who want to learn the Names of Allah but struggle with retention using traditional methods (books, flashcard decks). Tech-comfortable, likely iPhone users.
- **Secondary**: Parents and teachers looking for a trustworthy, ad-free Islamic learning tool to recommend.
- **Tertiary**: New Muslims or those reconnecting with their faith who want structured, accessible Islamic learning.

## Brand Voice

- **Warm and sincere**, never preachy or salesy. The app serves a spiritual purpose; the marketing should reflect that sincerity.
- **Confident but humble**. We know the app is good, but the focus is on the user's journey, not the product.
- **Clear and direct**. No jargon, no fluff. Respect the user's time.
- **Respectful of the sacred content**. The Names of Allah are treated with reverence, not as mere "flashcard content."

## Anti-References

- Generic SaaS landing pages with gradient heroes and metric dashboards
- Overly playful/gamified Islamic apps that trivialise the content
- Dark, moody "productivity tool" aesthetics
- Pages that feel like they're selling something aggressively

## Strategic Principles

- The page should feel like an invitation, not a sales pitch
- Simplicity over complexity; the app does one thing well
- Trust signals matter (scholarly verification, no ads, no subscriptions)
- The spiritual significance should come through in the design, not just the words
